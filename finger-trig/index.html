SETTINGS

Gesture: angutha + tarjani (index) khol kar angle banao. Dusre haath ki ✊ mutthi = angle lock/unlock.
Shortcuts: Space = lock · Q = quiz · D = degree/radian · ← → = angle (bina camera) · R = record · V = 9:16 · H = clean · Esc = band
Bina camera ke: unit circle par mouse se kheencho.

FINGER·TRIG

📐 ungliyon se trigonometry

Angutha aur ungli khol kar angle banao. Haath par tribhuj banega, aur sin, cos, tan live badalte dikhenge. Haath paas-door karke dekho, anupaat (ratio) wahi rehta hai!
